## At the OMA stand

::ShTextImg
---
positionText: left
spanText: m
alignText: left
urlImage: /images/events/sce-2026-booth-lwm2m-screen.jpg
altImage: Presenters at the OMA stand in front of the LwM2M ecosystem screen showing devices and a gateway connected to an LwM2M Server
title: |
  The LwM2M ecosystem, live demos and the team
text: |
  Visitors met OMA experts, saw an LwM2M Server link field devices to smart city and utility apps, and watched two live member demos.  

  **The LwM2M ecosystem.** The main booth panel covered native LwM2M devices (water meters, street lighting, environmental sensors, waste management) and gateways that bring in legacy protocols such as M-Bus, Modbus and OPC UA. It also presented the LwM2M Registry, the Water and Smart Cities application profiles, and conformance validation.  
  **LwM2M Client Conformance Tool.** Visitors learned how to test an LwM2M client against the OMA specifications and link the results to a product listing.  
  **Water 2.0.** Visitors got an early look at the vendor-neutral conformance profile for smart water metering, with its first release planned for March 2027.  
  **Talk to the team.** We shared LwM2M, Smart City and Water 2.0 material and met OMA members, utilities and city representatives.

**Live member demos.** Two OMA members showed LwM2M in action, one of them with a partner:
  - **AVSystem – Coiote.** Live LwM2M device management, turning device data into operational insight.
  - **Brno University of Technology with partner Lamberga – Smart Multiplatform Controller (SMC).** An edge controller for smart metering, smart city and critical infrastructure that maps meters, sensors and actuators to the OMA LwM2M object model. Supports LwM2M over CoAP or MQTT and wired or wireless connectivity (NB-IoT, 5G, Wi-SUN), with post-quantum-ready security. Developed in the EU co-funded IoT MeterNet project.

## Smart City SIG sessions

::ShTextImg
---
positionText: left
spanText: m
alignText: left
urlImage: /images/events/sce-2026-smart-city-sig-stage.jpg
altImage: A speaker presenting the Smart Cities SIG approach, from municipal needs to interoperable solutions
title: |
  From municipal needs to interoperable solutions
subtitle: |
  OMA Smart City SIG with Madrid Digital · 3–4 November 2026
text: |
  Together with **Madrid Digital**, the OMA Smart City SIG presented how municipal needs, written in plain language, are translated into standard LwM2M objects and Smart Data Models ready for digital twins.  
  **Session 1: Methodology for Interoperability.** Speakers: Juan Jesús Muñoz (Madrid City Council Digital Office), Joaquín Prado (OMA), Alberto Abella (FIWARE) and Vittorio Pettonati (AIOTI). The SIG's first open session. Using public irrigation as the use case, the group prioritised city needs, selected standardised OMA data objects and linked them to Smart Data Models, giving digital twins validated data from measured and inferred values.  
  **Session 2: Public Water Management Profile Handover.** Speakers: Kinga Stachura (AVSystem), Vittorio Pettonati, Alberto Abella, Juan Jesús Muñoz and Joaquín Prado. Existing LwM2M meter, valve and sensor objects can model city watering needs. Key lesson: an open valve does not prove water was delivered, so measured, estimated and simulated values must stay distinct.  
  **Session 3: Public Street Lighting Profile Handover.** Speakers: Juan Jesús Muñoz, Joaquín Prado, Carlo Bernocco (Paradox Engineering), Alberto Abella and Vittorio Pettonati. 14 semantic capabilities were defined and tested on 14 Madrid user stories, all checked against the OMA LwM2M Registry, with recommendations drafted for the OMA working group. Partners: Paradox Engineering, FIWARE, AIOTI, Universidad Politécnica de Madrid and the Madrid City Council Digital Office.
