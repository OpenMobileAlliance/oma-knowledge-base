  Thank you to everyone who visited the OMA stand, joined our sessions and supported us at **Smart City Expo World Congress** in Barcelona. ## At the OMA stand

::ShSegment
---
ui:
  wrapper: "bg-gray-50 dark:bg-slate-800 rounded-2xl shadow p-6 my-8"
---

**Hall 3, Stand F90**

- **Live member demos.** Two OMA members showed LwM2M in action, one of them with a partner:
  - **AVSystem – Coiote.** Live LwM2M device management, turning device data into operational insight.
  - **Brno University of Technology with partner Lamberga – Smart Multiplatform Controller (SMC).** An edge controller for smart metering, smart city and critical infrastructure that maps meters, sensors and actuators to the OMA LwM2M object model. Supports LwM2M over CoAP or MQTT and wired or wireless connectivity (NB-IoT, 5G, Wi-SUN), with post-quantum-ready security. Developed in the EU co-funded IoT MeterNet project.
- **LwM2M Client Conformance Tool.** Visitors learned how to test an LwM2M client against the OMA specifications and link the results to a product listing.
- **Water 2.0.** Visitors got an early look at the vendor-neutral conformance profile for smart water metering, with its first release planned for March 2027.
- **Talk to the team.** We shared LwM2M, Smart City and Water 2.0 material and met many OMA members, utilities and city representatives.

### From Pilots to City-Scale: Standards-Based Connectivity for Water Management

**3 November 2026 · Enabling Technologies session · AI X Cities stage**

Following the October 2026 LwM2M Water Interoperability Readiness Workshop, the OMA Utility Working Group brought the smart water metering conversation to Barcelona. The panel covered why utilities need multi-vendor interoperability, how to manage meters over a 15–20 year lifecycle, and where **Water 2.0** stands on its path to a first release in **March 2027**.

**Moderator:** Paul Wilson

**Panelists:**

- **Olivier Carmona**, Weepiez, Chair of the OMA Utility WG
- **Kinga Stachura**, New Business Manager, AVSystem
- **Pavel Mašek**, Associate Professor, Brno University of Technology
- **Matt Gillmore**, Itron
